## What breaks, and what to do

| What breaks | Why | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Additive spacing | On a 4 or 8 grid, 8 + 8 = 16 is a token. On the ladder at u = 1 it is not: the nearest rung is 13. Halving and doubling do not stay on the ladder. | Use the rule that does hold: adjacent rungs add up to the next (5 + 8 = 13, 8 + 13 = 21). Replace "two of these" with "the next rung". Where a sum must be exact, use `gap` rather than adding paddings. |
| Baseline rhythm | A 4-unit baseline grid is not kept at u = 1. | Use the u = 4 profile, or let unitless leading set the rhythm. |
| Device pixels | At 1.5× density, odd rungs at u = 1 (1, 3, 5, 13, 21, 55, 89 and so on) land on half device pixels. | Use u = 4 or u = 8, or accept the browser's rounding; borders and hairlines are not affected. |
| Icons | A 16 or 24 icon no longer fills a 13 or 21 box exactly. | Keep icon tokens exempt by role and centre the icon in a 21 or 34 box. |
| Hierarchy | Tokens that merge lose the difference between them; see the merge lists above. A 4-based scale loses the most between 28 and 44 at u = 1. | Add overrides (FP-SNAP-07) to move tokens of a merged group to neighbouring rungs, keeping order; or use u = 4, which merges less; or keep the area in Overlay. |
| Snapshots | Visual-regression baselines change everywhere a token moves. | Re-baseline once, in the same change as the Snap table. |
