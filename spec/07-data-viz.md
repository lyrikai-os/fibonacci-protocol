# 7. Data visualisation (FP-4b)

*Lyrikai: Fibonacci Protocol for UI/UX Design Agents · version 1.0.0-rc.5 · MIT License · [www.lyrikai.com](https://www.lyrikai.com)* · [Index](index.md)

In a chart, the data decides. φ and the ladder may shape the frame around the data. They never shape the data.

Rules marked Guard are normative in version 1.0, apply to any system that shows charts, and are tested by self-report with evidence. The rest of this section is informative.

## The data-honesty guard

### FP-VIZ-01 · φ frames, never data

φ, Fibonacci numbers and the ladder MAY set a chart's container ratio, margins, gaps and legend spacing. They MUST NOT set a scale, a tick position, a bar's length or thickness, an area that encodes a value, or a baseline.

- **Level:** Guard · **Status:** normative · **Applies when:** has: charts · **Test:** self-report, `viz.frames-not-data` · **Evidence:** convention (protocol choice)


### FP-VIZ-04 · Bars start at zero and are equally wide

A bar's length encodes its value, so a bar chart's value axis MUST start at zero, and bars in one series MUST have equal thickness. A line chart MAY start its axis elsewhere, with the axis labelled.

- **Level:** Guard · **Status:** normative · **Applies when:** has: charts · **Test:** self-report, `viz.bars` · **Evidence:** convention; basis: [cited source](https://ar5iv.arxiv.org/html/1907.02035)


### FP-VIZ-05 · No bent scales, no blended measures

A scale MUST NOT be broken, bent or truncated for appearance. Different measures MUST NOT be summed or blended into one mark.

- **Level:** Guard · **Status:** normative · **Applies when:** has: charts · **Test:** self-report, `viz.scales` · **Evidence:** convention (protocol choice)


### FP-VIZ-06 · Not measured is not zero

A value that has not been measured MUST be shown as not measured (an empty mark and words), never as 0 or 0%. Sample or placeholder data MUST be labelled as such (FP-HONEST-05).

- **Level:** Guard · **Status:** normative · **Applies when:** has: charts · **Test:** self-report, `viz.not-measured` · **Evidence:** convention (protocol choice)


## Frames, ticks and aspect

### FP-VIZ-02 · The data sets the aspect

A chart container MAY use 1.618 : 1 or a pair of rungs such as 34 : 21. Where the data calls for another aspect (for example to keep line slopes readable), the data's aspect MUST win. The research on the best aspect for slope reading does not agree, so no single aspect is prescribed.

- **Level:** FP-4b · **Status:** informative · **Applies when:** has: charts · **Test:** self-report, `viz.aspect` · **Evidence:** contested ([source](https://www.tableau.com/research/publications/empirical-model-slope-ratio-comparisons))


### FP-VIZ-03 · Nice-number ticks

Axis ticks SHOULD fall on 1, 2 or 5 times a power of ten. Ticks MUST NOT be placed on Fibonacci numbers for the sake of the protocol.

- **Level:** FP-4b · **Status:** informative · **Applies when:** has: charts · **Test:** machine, `viz.ticks` · **Evidence:** convention; basis: [cited source](https://www.ri.cmu.edu/publications/nice-numbers-for-graph-labels)


## Colour

### FP-VIZ-07 · Sequential colour

A sequential colour ramp SHOULD rise or fall in lightness without reversing and SHOULD be perceptually uniform: use an established map such as viridis, or build the ramp in OKLCH.

- **Level:** FP-4b · **Status:** informative · **Applies when:** has: charts · **Test:** self-report, `viz.sequential` · **Evidence:** convention; basis: [cited source](https://bids.github.io/colormap/)


### FP-VIZ-08 · Categorical colour

Categorical hues MAY be generated by stepping the hue by the golden angle in OKLCH at fixed lightness and chroma. This is a convention for spreading hues, not a perceptual guarantee: the result MUST be checked for colour-vision deficiency, and every category MUST also carry a label or a shape (FP-A11Y-14).

- **Level:** FP-4b · **Status:** informative · **Applies when:** has: charts · **Test:** self-report, `viz.categorical` · **Evidence:** convention


## Marks and small charts

### FP-VIZ-09 · Bar gaps and ends

The gap between bars or bands SHOULD be a rung in platform units. Bar thickness comes from the band scale (plot size divided among the bars) and is never a ladder value. A corner radius of 2 MAY be used only on the end away from the baseline.

- **Level:** FP-4b · **Status:** informative · **Applies when:** has: charts · **Test:** self-report, `viz.bar-gaps` · **Evidence:** convention (protocol choice)


### FP-VIZ-10 · Stat tiles

A stat tile SHOULD show its value at a display step and its label at step −1 or larger, with the unit and the period stated.

- **Level:** FP-4b · **Status:** informative · **Applies when:** has: charts · **Test:** self-report, `viz.stat-tile` · **Evidence:** convention (protocol choice)


### FP-VIZ-11 · Sparklines

A sparkline SHOULD have no axes, SHOULD label its last value, and MUST share one scale with every sparkline it is meant to be compared with.

- **Level:** FP-4b · **Status:** informative · **Applies when:** has: charts · **Test:** self-report, `viz.sparkline` · **Evidence:** convention (protocol choice)


### FP-VIZ-12 · Dot fields

A unit chart (one dot per item) MAY arrange its dots by the Vogel spiral (FP-ART-02). The count of dots MUST be the real count.

- **Level:** FP-4b · **Status:** informative · **Applies when:** has: charts · **Test:** self-report, `viz.dot-field` · **Evidence:** convention (protocol choice); basis: [cited source](https://doi.org/10.1016/0025-5564(79)90080-4)
