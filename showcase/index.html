Lyrikai: Fibonacci Protocol for UI/UX Design Agents

One small set of numbers and one ratio, used the same way everywhere. This page is built on dist/fp.css and shows each area of the protocol in use.

Demonstration content. Every name, figure and state on this page is a sample made for the demonstration. None of it is real data. The protocol is a convention for internal consistency; φ is a design convention here, not a law, and nothing on this page shows that a design using it is better. See the evidence page.

The φ split

A two-part layout whose parts have unequal weight and both hold content may split 61.8 to 38.2, written 1.618fr 1fr (the tokens --fp-split-major and --fp-split-minor) and declared with --fp-layout: phi-split. From 987 px wide the two panels below use it; below that they stack in one column.

Type scale and leading

Body is 16 px (1rem), the web default and a minimum. Each step is the body size times φk/2, rounded, written in rem, so it follows the reader's text size. Leading follows the role of the text first and its size second, and is unitless.

Controls, targets and focus

Protocol controls are at least the ladder target: 55 px for touch and for any coarse or unknown pointer, 34 px where the only pointer is fine. The floor behind this is 44 px (a protocol choice) or WCAG's 24 px at AA. The dashed square below is --fp-target at its true size. Press Tab to see the focus ring: a gap of 2 and a ring of 3, never removed.

The eight-state grammar

A state is shown by a form first, then a word, then optionally a hue. The forms differ by silhouette (what is filled, what is open, how many parts), so they can be told apart at small sizes and without colour. Every mark here has its word beside it. Tick the box to remove all hue and check this for yourself.

  • Openavailable, nothing happening. Form: hollow ring.
  • Attentionwaiting on the person. Form: solid disc inside a ring.
  • Activein progress. Form: solid disc.
  • Waitingwaiting on something else. Form: ring broken into 4 arcs.
  • Reviewready to be checked. Form: horizontal bar.
  • Donefinished and accepted. Form: solid disc with a check, in ink.
  • Blockedcannot continue. Form: ring with a bar struck through.
  • Quietoff or dormant. Form: small centred dot, no ring.

Rules: FP-STATE-01 to FP-STATE-09, FP-A11Y-14. Evidence: convention (protocol choice). In version 1.0 this grammar is informative guidance, not a tested claim. Marks are 21 with a stroke of 2; chips grow with their text.

Data visualisation

In a chart the data decides. φ and the ladder may shape the frame, never the data. This sample bar chart has a frame of 610 by 377 (two rungs) and margins on rungs. The scale starts at zero, bars are equally wide and their width comes from the plot size, and the ticks fall on 1, 2 or 5 times a power of ten (here steps of 20), not on Fibonacci numbers.

Generated art

The golden angle and Fibonacci spiral counts are measured in plant growth, but they are not universal: in one study of 657 sunflowers, 74% of spiral counts were Fibonacci numbers and about 18% were not.

How this was made

A Vogel spiral: the n-th dot sits at radius 14 × √n and at angle n × 137.50776°, the golden angle computed from its closed form. 144 dots placed, from 144 asked for. The same inputs give the same file; nothing here is random and nothing moves.

The spiral arms you can see are a result of the placement. They are not drawn. This is sample art, not evidence about nature.

Rules: FP-ART-01 to FP-ART-11 (informative in 1.0). Evidence: cited for the golden angle and Vogel's model. The SVG has a viewBox, an accessible name, no script and no external files.

Platform anchors

The ladder is in platform units: 1 u is 1 CSS px, 1 pt or 1 dp. Platform defaults and accessibility floors sit outside the scale and always win. In version 1.0 only the web is normative; the others are published as guidance with their anchors and floors stated.
